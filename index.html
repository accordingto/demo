Welcome to Reading Club

Read English articles, tap any word to hear it, and build your own vocabulary list.

Quick start

  1. 1
    Create an articlePaste your own text, import a web page, or let the AI write one.
  2. 2
    Read and tap wordsClick a word to hear it. Double-click to save it to your vocabulary.
  3. 3
    Save itPress Save to keep the article and its words in your Library.

The menu (left side)

  • GuideThis page. It opens first, unless you turn on “Open the Library first” in Reading settings.
  • CreateStart a new article: Paste text, From a link, or AI generate (a few per day).
  • ArticleThe article you are reading. It is grayed out until you open or create one.
  • LibraryYour saved articles. Double-click a title to open it, click a column heading to sort.
  • UsersOnly the site owner sees this: add users, set passwords, disable or delete accounts.
  • Reading settingsTheme, text size, font, spacing, voice and speed. The first switch there, “Open the Library first”, chooses which page opens first.
  • LanguageSwitch the whole site between English and 中文.
  • Access codeType it and press Enter. A green light shows who you are; a red light means you are not signed in.

  1. 1 Read aloud reads the whole article. Click it again (or press the Space bar) to pause and resume.
  2. 2 Edit turns the article into an editor — see “Edit and save” below.
  3. 3 Settings opens theme, text size, font, line spacing and voice settings.
  4. 4 Paragraph button reads from this paragraph all the way to the end.
  5. 5Click a word once to hear it. It lights up for a moment so you know which word you tapped.
  6. 6Double-click a word to add it to your Vocabulary. It turns highlighted everywhere in the article. Select two to six words to add a phrase.
  7. 7Vocabulary card: click it to hear the word and jump to it in the text; click again to jump to the next place; double-click to hear the whole sentence. The × removes the word.

Edit and save

  1. 1
    Press the pencil at the top right of the article.
  2. 2
    Change anythingClick the title, the text or a question and type.
  3. 3
    Save changesPress “Save changes” to apply your edits (or Cancel to discard them).
  4. 4
    Press Save The green Save button keeps the article and its vocabulary in your Library. New words are also saved automatically.

Press Share to get a link that anyone can open — they can read and look up words without an access code.
